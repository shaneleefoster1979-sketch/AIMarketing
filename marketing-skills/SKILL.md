# AI Marketing Skills

Priming pack for direct-response marketing work: copy, funnels, email, paid ads, lead magnets,
content strategy, and analytics. Load this whenever the task involves writing marketing copy,
designing a funnel or campaign, planning a launch, or reviewing marketing performance.

## When to load what

| If the task involves...                          | Read this first              | Then read                          |
|----------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Any marketing task at all                          | `core-principles.md`          | —                                     |
| Planning a funnel, offer, or launch sequence        | `the-fundamentals.md`         | relevant playbook(s) below            |
| Website copy, ads, headlines, hooks                 | `playbooks/copywriting.md`    | `about.md` for voice                  |
| A long-form sales page or VSL script                | `playbooks/sales-letters.md`  | `playbooks/copywriting.md`            |
| Email sequences, newsletters, nurture flows         | `playbooks/email-marketing.md`| `the-fundamentals.md`                 |
| Paid acquisition (Meta, Google, YouTube, etc.)       | `playbooks/paid-ads.md`       | `playbooks/copywriting.md`            |
| Freebies, opt-ins, quizzes, challenges               | `playbooks/lead-magnets.md`   | `the-fundamentals.md`                 |
| Content calendars, organic/social strategy           | `playbooks/content-strategy.md`| `about.md`                           |
| Promoting third-party products/brands for commission | `playbooks/affiliate-marketing.md` | `the-fundamentals.md`, `playbooks/email-marketing.md` |
| Reporting, metrics, and deciding what to cut/scale   | `playbooks/analytics.md`      | —                                     |

## How to use this pack

1. Always start with `core-principles.md` — it's short and everything else assumes it.
2. Identify which stage of the funnel (see `the-fundamentals.md`) the task lives in, and load
   the matching playbook(s) from the table above.
3. Check `about.md` before producing any customer-facing copy so voice and positioning stay
   consistent across channels.
4. If this pack is paired with a `memory-vault/`, write durable decisions (offer positioning,
   audience definitions, what's been tested and what won) back into `MEMORY.md` so future
   sessions don't relitigate them.

## Non-goals

This pack is about marketing strategy, copy, and analysis — not product engineering, legal
review of claims, or ad-platform account administration. Flag those as separate concerns
rather than guessing at them.
