# Playbook: Content Strategy

Applies to organic content, social media, blogging/SEO, and editorial calendars. Assumes
`core-principles.md` and `about.md` are already loaded.

## Content's job in the funnel

Organic content mostly serves **awareness** and **nurture** (see `the-fundamentals.md`) — it
builds an audience and trust over time rather than closing sales directly. Don't judge
individual pieces of content by immediate sales; judge the system by audience growth,
engagement, and its contribution to the lead-magnet and offer stages downstream.

## Content pillars

Define 3–5 recurring themes tied directly to the offer's core promise, so content builds
toward the sale instead of wandering. A useful split:

1. **Problem-awareness content** — makes the audience recognize a problem they weren't
   naming correctly ("You don't have a discipline problem, you have a systems problem").
2. **Authority/proof content** — case studies, results, behind-the-scenes of the mechanism
   working.
3. **Objection-handling content** — addresses the specific reasons people hesitate, before
   they ever see a pitch.
4. **Community/relatability content** — personality, story, and points of connection that
   make the brand a "who," not a "what."
5. **Direct-response content** — occasional, clearly-labeled offers, kept a minority of the
   calendar so trust isn't eroded.

## Format and platform fit

- Match format to the platform's native consumption pattern instead of repurposing verbatim —
  a script written for talking-head video needs different pacing than a carousel or a blog
  post, even covering the same idea.
- Hook (first line/first 3 seconds) matters more than production quality on almost every
  platform — see `copywriting.md` for hook formulas.
- Repurpose one core idea across formats rather than generating unrelated ideas per platform;
  this keeps messaging consistent and makes production sustainable.

## Cadence

Consistency beats burst-and-disappear publishing for audience-building. Set a cadence the
business can sustain indefinitely, not the maximum theoretically possible for one motivated
week.

## Measuring content

- Track retention/watch-through and saves/shares as leading indicators of resonance, not just
  likes.
- Track content's downstream contribution (opt-ins attributed, traffic to offer pages) not
  just on-platform engagement — engagement without downstream movement is a sign the content
  and the funnel are disconnected.
- See `analytics.md` for full metric definitions and reporting cadence.
